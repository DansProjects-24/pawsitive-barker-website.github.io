About Me

Our Services

Walking

Daily adventures for your dog to stretch their legs, sniff, and socialise! Our fun group walks take place in secure local fields — perfect for safe play, exercise, and tail-wagging joy.

Day Care

A full day of care, cuddles, and companionship while you’re busy. Your dog will enjoy walks, playtime, and plenty of rest in a relaxed, home-style environment.

Overnight Stays

Going away? We’ll make sure your pet feels right at home. Overnight stays include walks, meals, and love — keeping your furry friend safe, happy, and comfortable.

Drop-Ins

Quick visits for feeding, playtime, or bathroom breaks — ideal for puppies, cats, or dogs who prefer the comfort of home. Reliable, friendly care when you can’t be there.

Adventure Days

Extra-special outings for dogs who love a bigger adventure! From sand barn play sessions and safe swimming to exciting beach trips, these days are all about exploring, enrichment, and having fun with friends.
